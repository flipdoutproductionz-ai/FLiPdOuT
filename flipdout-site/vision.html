Vision & Systems

Break The Silence.

FLiPdOuT Productions exists to turn flight simulation into a living aviation experience through crew presence, operational depth, and immersive systems.

Mission Statement

Break The Silence.

Modern flight simulators are visually stunning and technically incredible, but once the aircraft leaves the gate, something is often missing. The world feels quiet. FLiPdOuT Productions was created to bring that missing layer to life.

What we believe

People do not just want to fly an airplane. They want to experience aviation.

What we build

A living ecosystem of modules that add immersion without compromising the pilot’s control.

How we think

Foundation first. Modular systems. Human-centered AI. Stable growth. Long-term sustainability.

Founder

Andy Hicks — FLiP

Andy grew up fascinated by aviation, spent thousands of hours in flight simulators, and noticed the silence that can exist in modern commercial sim experiences. FLiPdOuT was born from the desire to change that and create something more alive.

The FLiP Legendary System

The heart of the ecosystem is the FLiP HUB.

FLiP HUB

The central platform that reads simulator telemetry and distributes information to connected modules.

Telemetry Bridge

Lightweight, modular, expandable, and designed to keep the system responsive and stable.

Diagnostics & Updates

Built to support health monitoring, community updates, and future expansion support.

Integrated Systems

Five systems. One ecosystem.

System One — ATC Copilot

Crew interaction, callouts, checklist support, flight phase awareness, and dynamic AI dialogue.

System Two — Cabin Crew & Passenger Experience

Briefings, cabin dialogue, passenger atmosphere, and in-flight human presence.

System Three — Ground Operations

Pushback, fueling, cargo, baggage, gate ops, and turnaround realism.

System Four — Flight Operations

Career persistence, virtual airline operations, pilot records, and company management.

System Five — Emergency Operations

Medical events, disturbances, system abnormalities, weather challenges, and operational decisions.

The Hidden Sixth Element — Airshow Mode

A special reward experience unlocked by the full ecosystem, built to celebrate the joy of flight.

Development Philosophy

Build the foundation first.

No single module should ever compromise the entire platform. Every system should stand on its own, stay isolated, and scale cleanly over time.
