Vision & Systems

Break The Silence.

FLiPdOuT Productions exists to turn flight simulation into a living aviation experience through crew presence, operational depth, and immersive systems. Right now, only FLiPs Flight Ops Companion is ready. The larger FLiP system is still in development.

Mission Statement

Break The Silence.

Modern flight simulators are visually stunning and technically incredible, but once the aircraft leaves the gate, something is often missing. The world feels quiet. FLiPdOuT Productions was created to bring that missing layer to life.
